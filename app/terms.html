Bindle+ subscriptions

  • Plans: Bindle+ is offered monthly ($3.99), quarterly ($10.99), and yearly ($39.99). Not every plan is always available to every account — see below.
  • Billing: subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and renew automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Manage or cancel anytime in your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Price changes and refunds: handled through Apple's App Store terms and policies. We don't process payments or refunds directly.
  • Repeated cancel-and-resubscribe: if an account starts and cancels the monthly plan 3 or more times within a rolling 365-day period, monthly billing is no longer offered to that account going forward — its cheapest available option becomes quarterly instead. This doesn't cancel or affect a subscription you already have active; it only applies the next time you'd otherwise start a new one.
  • Same features, different billing: Bindle+ unlocks the same things — unlimited memories, your vault synced across every device, shared vaults, and printable photo books — regardless of which billing period you choose.

Deleting memories and your account

Deleting a memory holds it for 30 days before it's permanently erased, in case you change your mind.

Deleting your account is immediate and permanent, with no recovery window. It erases your account data from our servers, disconnects your Apple ID, and removes your memories from your device, including the copies kept in your own iCloud. Cancel any active Bindle+ subscription separately through your Apple ID if you don't want it to keep renewing.

Full detail on what's collected and how deletion works is in our Privacy Policy.
