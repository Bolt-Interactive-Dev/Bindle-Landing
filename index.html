Are my family's files private?

Yes. Every space is private by default, and only people you invite can see or add to it.

What kinds of files can I keep?

Photos, videos, PDFs, scanned artwork, documents, letters, and more. All in the same space.

Will grandparents find it easy to use?

Yes. Invited family members open one clean feed with clear actions, no camera-roll clutter, no buried sharing menus.

Can I export everything later?

Yes. Every plan includes exports, so nothing is locked into Bindle.

Can different family members add memories?

Yes. Anyone you invite can add their own photos, documents, videos, and notes.

Is Bindle available on Android?

iPhone first. Android is planned — we'll update this page when it's ready.
